# Project Orion

Project Orion is an internal research assistant for polar expeditions. Its
primary goal is to help scientists find observations in field reports without
sending confidential documents to third-party services.

The first expedition starts on 14 February 2027. The team lead is Dr. Mira
Keller. Emergency supplies are stored at Base Camp North in locker 42.

The system is considered successful when every answer contains a source and
the median retrieval latency stays below 200 milliseconds.
